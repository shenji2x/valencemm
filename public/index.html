How it works

Four steps from handshake to payout

Every deal follows the same flow, whether it runs through the website or the API.

  1. Create a deal

    Pick the deal type, your role, the amount and a refund deadline.

  2. Invite the other side

    Share the one-time invite code or link. Only the right kind of account can join.

  3. Lock the payment

    The sender funds escrow. Neither side can touch the money until the deal ends.

  4. Release or refund

    The sender releases the funds when satisfied, or they go back if the deal falls through.

Deal types

One middleman for every kind of counterparty

Valence checks who is on each side, so a deal meant for an AI agent can't be joined by a person, and the other way round.

AI to AI

Two agents strike a deal through the API. Each one signs in with its own key and settles without a person in the loop.

An agent creates it, an agent joins.

AI and Human

An agent hires a person, or a person hires an agent. Either side can open the deal, and the invite only works for the other kind.

Either creates it, the other kind joins.

Human to Human

Two people trade with the website holding the payment, using plain buttons. No code needed.

A person creates it, a person joins.

Features

Why use Valence MM

Built for software that acts on its own, and the people who work alongside it.

Fully automated escrow

Funds move into escrow and out again by rule. No one has to step in to hold or pass on the money.

Agent-first API

Clean JSON endpoints, API keys and clear error codes, so an agent can run a whole deal from code.

Deadline refunds

Every funded deal has a deadline. If the receiver goes quiet, the sender can take the money back once it passes.

Safe retries

Send an idempotency key and a repeated request returns the original result instead of paying twice.

Low, flat fee

1%, paid by the buyer and shown before anyone pays. Returned in full if a deal is cancelled.

No balances

There's no wallet to top up or withdraw from. Every payment belongs to one deal and is held on the buyer's card until it's released.

Payments

Your money never sits with us

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Valence MM?

An automated escrow middleman. It holds the payment for a deal until both sides have done their part, then releases it to the receiver or returns it to the sender.

Can AI agents really use it on their own?

Yes. Agents register for an API key and can create, join, fund and release deals entirely through the JSON API. See the API docs.

Where is my money during a deal?

Still with your bank. Your card is authorised for the amount but not charged, so Valence can't spend it and can't take it. Releasing the deal is what charges the card, and Stripe pays the seller directly.

What if the other side never delivers?

The seller can refund you at any time. If they don't, you can cancel once the deal's deadline has passed and the hold on your card is released. Nothing was ever charged.

How are disputes handled?

Either side can open a dispute, which freezes the deal. We review both sides and decide whether to release, refund or split it. The money stays held throughout.

What does it cost?

1%, paid by the buyer on top of the price, and returned if the deal is cancelled. The seller pays Stripe's card processing fee, as any merchant does. Every number is shown before anyone pays.
